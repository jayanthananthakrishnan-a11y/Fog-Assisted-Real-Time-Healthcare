TOPIC 19
Title: Fog-Assisted Real-Time Healthcare / Smart-City Monitoring System (iFogSim)
Research Problem: Continuous health or city-sensor streams cannot tolerate high cloud latency and consume
excessive bandwidth if sent raw.
Research Gap: End-to-end design that combines filtering at fog with cloud analytics is a realistic 2025–27 use
case.
Proposed Solution: Model sensors, fog nodes and cloud. Place filtering/aggregation modules at fog and heavy
analytics at cloud. Measure latency, bandwidth saving and reliability in iFogSim.
Tools Required: iFogSim
